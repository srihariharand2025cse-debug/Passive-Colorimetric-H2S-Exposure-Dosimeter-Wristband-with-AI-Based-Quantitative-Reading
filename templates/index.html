About the Project: Passive Colorimetric H₂S Dosimeter System
Chemical Sensing Principle

Hydrogen Sulfide ($H_2S$) is a lethal, colorless toxic gas found in petrochemical refining, municipal sewage, and heavy industrial facilities. The wearable wristband utilizes a passive colorimetric sensing film coated with reactive metallic nanoparticles (e.g. Silver Nanoparticles or Lead Acetate).

Reaction Mechanism: 2Ag(s) + H₂S(g) → Ag₂S(s) + H₂(g) Upon gas contact, formation of dark silver sulfide causes quantifiable optical decay across Red, Green, and Blue intensity channels.

Environmental kinetics dictate that ambient temperature and relative humidity accelerate reaction rate, which the dual-model AI algorithm factors in for exact PPM reading.

Occupational Safety Thresholds (OSHA & ACGIH)
Risk Category H₂S Threshold Health Consequence & Safety Action
Low 0.0 - 19.9 ppm Detectable rotten-egg odor. Safe within routine 8-hr TWA limit.
Moderate 20.0 - 49.9 ppm Exceeds OSHA 20 ppm ceiling. Eye/respiratory irritation. Wear PPE.
High ≥ 50.0 ppm Severe olfactory fatigue, IDLH risk. Evacuate & don SCBA.
Dual-Model AI Architecture

The pipeline incorporates a Random Forest Regressor ($R^2 > 0.94$) to predict continuous cumulative exposure level in PPM, and a Random Forest Classifier (Accuracy $> 93\%$) to assign discrete risk categories. All inferences are stored in SQLite and streamed via Flask REST APIs.
